Sources and definitions

Sunshine is measured bright-sunshine duration, shown in hours per month. Station normals have different periods and station locations; Toronto’s available sunshine observations cover 1981–2001. Milan’s published historical station series and Zürich’s Fluntern normal are from compiled climate tables.

Rainy day means at least 1 mm of modeled daily rain. Snowy day means at least 1 cm of modeled daily fresh snowfall (light flurries excluded). Days can count in both categories. Temperature is the mean of modeled daily average temperatures. These three series are calculated from Open-Meteo’s ERA5 historical daily data for 1991–2020, at city-center coordinates; ERA5’s grid is about 25 km, so local terrain and airport values may differ. Days are averaged across 30 occurrences of each month. Years are totals for day counts and sunshine; temperature year is a day-weighted average.

Why sunshine has its own source: the ERA5 sunshine-duration field returned implausibly high totals in this extraction, so it is excluded. Treat rare-snow estimates and fine city rankings as approximate.

ERA5 documentation and underlying daily API: Open-Meteo historical weather. City-specific API links are included with each sunshine source below.
