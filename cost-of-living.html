What the model includes

Home. One 2 bedroom unit for two adults, 1 or 2 baths, choosing the cheaper suitable property type. Published city data provide 1 and 3 bedroom rents outside the centre, so the starting 2 bedroom rent is their midpoint. “Outside centre” does not prove a 20 minute drive to the core, pet acceptance, parking, or availability. Replace it with a real listing in an appropriate neighborhood.

Food. Two main meals a day for two people; no breakfast. On cooking days, grocery cost = four portions × grocery cost per portion. On dining days, one inexpensive lunch for each person and a midrange dinner for two. 30.44 days per month. Drinks, tips, delivery and taxes can change the bill. The grocery cost per portion is a planning assumption guided by the local market basket, not a surveyed meal price.

Maple. One mini dachshund: routine exam, vaccines/parasite prevention, food and modest routine-care reserve rolled into a monthly allowance. No emergency surgery or chronic illness, and no pet insurance. Values are editable planning allowances, not vet quotes.

Health. Routine public coverage in Spain, Italy and Ontario with a modest out-of-pocket allowance; Dutch basic premiums for two plus small routine costs; Swiss basic premiums for two plus a routine-cost reserve. No dental/vision, prescriptions, major care or employer supplement. Swiss premiums and deductibles vary substantially by age, plan and location. Public coverage requires eligibility as a resident/worker.

Jobs. Annual gross role targets are scenario assumptions; monthly net figures are approximate separate-earner outcomes after income tax, employee social charges and mandatory payroll pension contributions where applicable. Swiss health insurance and Dutch basic premiums stay in the medical line, not payroll deductions. Figures are annual net divided by 12, even if a contract pays 13 or 14 instalments. They exclude bonuses, equity, special immigrant tax breaks, optional pension contributions, and employer benefits. For Zürich, the starting CHF 120,000 + CHF 90,000 gross produces CHF 13,350 monthly net, an implied 23.7% combined payroll deduction. This assumes a married two-earner household, no children or church tax, and residence in the City of Zürich. The actual pension plan, accident premium and final joint tax assessment can change it. Salary availability and language requirements matter as much as the plotted budget. Toronto starts near your stated current combined pay for reference.

Currency. Bank of Canada daily indicative rates on 25 September 2026: €1 = C$1.6122; CHF1 = C$1.7072. Conversion is for side-by-side comparison, not a forecast or purchasing-power adjustment. Original amounts remain local in the editor.
